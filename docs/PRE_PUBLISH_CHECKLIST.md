# Pre-Publish Checklist: Google + AI Search (GEO)
*Mandatory quality, truth, crawlability, and citation audit to run on every article and money page before publishing to TheSmartMag.*

---

## 1. Truth and Trust (Must Pass Before Publication)
- [ ] Every price, rule, spec, and date is verified against an official primary source (official carrier, transport authority, or venue).
- [ ] No fabricated claims of "we tested", "we spent", or "receipts" unless real first-hand receipts and logs exist.
- [ ] Zero simulated ratings, fake reviews, mock testimonials, or invented view counters.
- [ ] Star ratings come with an explicit, documented methodology and criteria breakdown.
- [ ] Discount codes and promotional offers match the affiliate partner's active terms.
- [ ] Plain-language affiliate disclosure prominently displayed near the top above the first CTA.
- [ ] Author byline and real editorial bio displayed with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.
- [ ] For finance/trading topics: Educational-only disclaimer and risk disclosures prominently placed (no profit guarantees).

---

## 2. Answer-First Writing (Optimized for AI Search Citation: Perplexity, ChatGPT, Claude)
- [ ] 2 to 3 sentence direct, unequivocal answer situated at the top above the fold.
- [ ] Headings (H2/H3) formatted as real queries people ask (e.g., *"Is Pocket Wi-Fi Still Worth It in 2026?"*).
- [ ] At least one comprehensive comparison table comparing pricing, key features, and explicit "Who it's for / Who it's NOT for" columns.
- [ ] FAQ block with 4 to 6 concise, self-contained questions and direct answers.
- [ ] At least one original asset included: custom calculation table, screenshot, hands-on speed test, or transit receipt.
- [ ] Permanent outbound links to primary sources for variable details (carrier APN guides, entry rules).

---

## 3. On-Page SEO
- [ ] Targeted primary keyword in H1, SEO Title, and first 100 words.
- [ ] SEO Title Tag strictly under 60 characters with high-CTR hook `(2026)`.
- [ ] Meta description under 155 characters summarizing the direct value proposition.
- [ ] Clean canonical URL slug on root domain (e.g., `thesmartmag.com/travel/...` or `thesmartmag.com/blog/...`).
- [ ] Single H1 tag per page with descending logical hierarchy (H2 > H3).
- [ ] 3 to 5 contextual internal links to related destination hubs, travel tech, or store gear.
- [ ] Compressed responsive imagery (WebP/AVIF) with descriptive, non-stuffed alt text.
- [ ] Canonical tag strictly points to the singular root master URL.

---

## 4. Crawlability & Machine Parsing
- [ ] Server-Side Rendered (SSR) HTML: Full article copy visible in page source without requiring client-side JS hydration.
- [ ] Included in `https://thesmartmag.com/sitemap.xml` and linked from parent category hubs.
- [ ] Unblocked in `robots.txt` for `Googlebot`, `Bingbot`, `OAI-SearchBot`, `PerplexityBot`, `Claude-SearchBot`, and `ChatGPT-User`.
- [ ] Zero interstitial pop-ups or registration gates blocking mobile reading view.
- [ ] URL inspection in Google Search Console confirms full DOM parsing.

---

## 5. Speed, Usability & Affiliate Health
- [ ] Fast mobile rendering (<2.5s LCP, zero layout shifts).
- [ ] Pre-allocated height containers for comparison tables and affiliate cards.
- [ ] High-contrast, mobile-friendly CTA buttons with clean destination URLs.
- [ ] Geo-targeted offers routed cleanly via Amazon OneLink / Travelpayouts project verification.

---

## 6. Post-Publish Verification (Days 1–30)
- [ ] Day 1: Submit URL to Google Search Console (Request Indexing) and Bing Webmaster Tools (IndexNow).
- [ ] Day 7: Inspect Google Search Console for impressions, ranking queries, and initial indexing status.
- [ ] Day 30: Query ChatGPT Search, Perplexity AI, Claude, and Gemini with target long-tail queries; record citations.
- [ ] Day 30: Check referral sessions in analytics from `chatgpt.com`, `perplexity.ai`, and `gemini.google.com`.
- [ ] Recurring: Review and update timestamp whenever pricing, train timetables, or carrier policies change.

---

## 🚨 Strict Red Flags (Immediate Disqualification)
- Content generated from a generic template with no original analysis.
- Unverifiable testing claims or fake verification badges.
- Inaccurate pricing or hidden fees not stated up front.
- Links to expired or non-compliant affiliate campaigns.
